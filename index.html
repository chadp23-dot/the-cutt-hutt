Meet the Shop

One Shop. One Owner. Scott Dickerson.

The Cutt Hutt is a small wooden shop on N Lynnbank Road with THE CUTT HUTT in big white letters, barber pole lanterns by the door, and a neon OPEN BARBER sign in the window. A window sign says it plain: BARBER SHOP, WALK-INS WELCOME.

Scott describes his own shop as “a family and friendly atmosphere, where everyone is welcome, all ages, and walk ins.” You deal with the owner himself, the man whose name is on the card, not a stranger behind a counter.
